Shelf Sync

Three fixes for orders that hit an out-of-stock item on a delivery app: keep online stock in sync with the shelf, warn the store before an item runs out, and offer substitutes that fit what the customer wants.

Try itPlace the vegan shopper's order with every fix off. The plant burgers and oat drink are listed online, but the shelf is empty. Then switch the fixes on and press Reset store to compare.

Simulation

What each fix changes over 300 orders

Each scenario runs the same 300 simulated orders five times and shows the average. The scenario that matches your switches is in bold.

Assumptions, all made up for illustration: one grocery store with 13 items; 25% of shoppers are vegan and 15% gluten-free; each order has 1–3 items; the store restocks every 25 orders; on about one order in four, an item leaves the shelf without the system knowing (damage, miscount, walk-in sale); stores act on 60% of low-stock alerts; 85% of shoppers accept a substitute that fits their preference and 10% accept one that does not. Use it to compare the fixes, not to predict real figures.
